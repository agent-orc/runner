# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Give and accept feedback based on the work.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and their impact.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, and publishing another person's private information are not
acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project spaces and when representing the project in public.
Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through a private
contact method listed on their GitHub profiles. Maintainers will respect the
privacy and security of reporters. Enforcement follows the guidelines in the
Contributor Covenant 2.1.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1, available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
